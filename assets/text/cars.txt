# What each kind of vehicle is like: what its card says when you click one in World mode. Edit freely, then reload the page.
# Each [section] is a vehicle type, named as in assets/models/Cars.glb. Under it, `attribute = value` lines:
#
#   name    what it's called on its card (followed by its own number, like "Police car #3")
#   mood    its current mood, usually an emoji. The mood carries the traits that set how the car drives: `speed` scales its
#           speed, `boost` scales its boost when driven with shift held, `braking` scales how hard it brakes and `control` how
#           sharply it steers when driven (1 is the standard car; 2 is twice as much), and `weight` scales how much a car
#           slows when it crashes into this one (heavier, more slow-down; 1 is standard)
#   loves   what it loves. A love (or hate, or the mood) can also carry `legendary` or `terrible`: the car
#           shines gold and sparkles the more legendary it is (a shifting rainbow instead, at 2 legendary and 0 terrible),
#           or hops and smokes heavily the more terrible it is
#   hates   what it hates
#   vanitychance   how likely a vehicle of that type is to wear one of its plates, from 0 (never) to 1 (always)
#   plate   a vanity registration (up to nine characters, capitals, digits, spaces and dashes) it might wear instead of a
#           normal one. Give it as many lines as you like: a type with none of its own uses [default]'s
#
# Give an attribute several lines to have each vehicle of that type pick one of them (always the same one for the same
# vehicle). Anything a type leaves out comes from [default], which is also used for any type that isn't listed.
